Mac-10

By Kieran Airey

This Mac-10 is a game ready sub-machine gun asset that I created using Autodesk Maya. This personal project gave me the opportunity to develop my hard surface modelling skills which were improved throughout the duration of the project. The main inspiration for the weapon was after seeing a similar variant of the Hotline Miami texture on a Mac-10 in 'Payday 3'. Following this I used references to create a direct replica of a Mac-10 with a unique texture. I also used industry standard skills when texturing and rendering this weapon within Substance Painter and Marmoset Toolbag 5.
