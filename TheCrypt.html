The Crypt

By Kieran Airey

The crypt is a personal project of mine and my first experience creating a medieval style environment. By using a modular workflow I was able to construct a full scene including a main entrance with a door and stairs leading down into a central cross junction, which continues into a main crypt area. The environment is fully playable and includes a series of props set dressed throughout the world. During the project I was able to build upon my industry standard skills, gaining more knowledge and experience when creating immersive, optimised game environments.
