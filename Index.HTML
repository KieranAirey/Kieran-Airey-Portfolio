About Me

I’m a 3D environment and prop artist currently graduating from Games Art at Teesside University, where I have developed my skills over the past 3 years. I have 5 years of total experience creating game-ready 3D assets and environments within game engines. This has allowed me to build a strong understanding of AAA pipelines and industry-standard workflows. My key skills include environment art, optimisation, prop creation, hard-surface modelling, shader creation, photogrammetry, and sculpting. I am a highly motivated individual with a strong passion for building optimised, visually striking, and immersive game worlds with a strong drive to improve upon my skills.
