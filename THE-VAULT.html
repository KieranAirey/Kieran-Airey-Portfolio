THE-VAULT

By Kieran Airey

The vault is my third year final project and is a ‘Fallout 76’ inspired vault. This 3D real time environment was built with Unreal Engine 5 using industry standard pipelines and production methods. The scene takes place within vault 76 and includes a corridor, utility area, living room and bedroom. Throughout this project I was able to develop and build upon my skills as an environment artist. For the project I was able to successfully represent the retro-futuristic art style visually seen throughout the Fallout franchise. The project allowed me to improve upon my Industry standard skills comprising of but not limited to modular workflows, shader creation, physically based rendering and high to low poly baking.
